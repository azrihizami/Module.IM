Induction Motor Trainer · Curriculum

Five modules, one motor

Five modules follow one induction motor through one CDIO cycle. In Induction, trainees predict how the kit motor will perform. In Build and Wind they make it. In Drive they run it and measure it against the prediction.

Register interestThe trainer
The series

How the modules fit together

Each module states the kit state it starts from and the state it leaves. A module taken on its own starts from a prepared kit and a one-page recap.

  1. 01MTM-01FoundationFields, iron, magnets, induction and force, in FEMM and on the bench
  2. 02MTM-02-IMInductionOpen the kit motor and predict its torque, current and efficiency
  3. 03MTM-03-IMBuildStack the core, fit the cage rotor, assemble a motor that turns freely
  4. 04MTM-04WindDesign, wind, insert and test the three-phase stator winding
  5. 05MTM-05-IMDrivePower it up from DOL, star-delta and a VFD, and measure it

Every session

Brief, Explore, Do, Check

  1. BriefThe trainer's slides: the idea and its equations
  2. ExploreThe module's app: a model you can change
  3. DoThe kit or the bench: make it, measure it
  4. CheckYour prediction against the result, and a short quiz

Each module has four or five sessions. The trainer's slides, the trainee's workbook and the course hub use the same four steps, and every value goes into the Motor Logbook with its data label: Confirmed, Inferred, Training target or Not available.
