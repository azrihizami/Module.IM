Complete Training Curriculum

Five comprehensive modules progressing from fundamental electromagnetic principles to complete motor manufacturing and testing — designed to develop industry-ready engineering technologists through hands-on learning.

1

Theory Foundation

Theoretical Foundation

Master electromechanical energy conversion principles and induction motor theory essential for understanding how motors work.

  • Core electromagnetic concepts
  • Faraday's law and Lorentz force concepts
  • Rotating magnetic field principles
  • Equivalent circuit of induction machine
Learning Outcomes: Understand electromagnetic principles governing induction motor operation and analyse motor performance using equivalent circuit models
2

Core Lamination Design

Design & Simulation

Learn about the stator core and rotor design, their materials and manufacturing process — including finite element simulation for design optimisation.

  • Core lamination design and issues
  • Rotor design and issues
  • Manufacturing methods for core
  • Design optimization methods
  • FEMM electromagnetic simulation
Learning Outcomes: Design and evaluate stator/rotor lamination geometry; use simulation tools to optimise core performance
🔬 Includes Simulation Exercises (Electromagnetic Simulator App)
3

Stator Winding

Hands-On Manufacturing

Learn motor winding design and build your own stator winding using IEC standard winding diagrams and professional winding assistant tools.

  • Understanding winding parameters
  • Use our winding assistant apps to build coils
  • Use IEC standard winding diagrams
  • Learn to estimate copper length and mass
Learning Outcomes: Design a three-phase stator winding layout, wind coils to specification, and estimate material requirements
🔧 Full Winding Construction Experience (Winding Assistant App)
4

Mechanical Assembly

Hands-On Assembly

Complete and test the full mechanical assembly of the induction motor — from rotor installation to terminal connections.

  • Understanding the issues of mechanical assembly of motor
  • Balancing and mounting the rotor, alignment
  • Complete the motor winding connection to terminals
  • Quality inspection and verification
Learning Outcomes: Assemble a complete induction motor from components, verify mechanical integrity, and connect winding terminations correctly
🔩 Complete Motor Assembly
5

Drive System Integration

Advanced Application

Connect the assembled motor to various drive systems and test real-world operating conditions — including safety procedures.

  • DOL (Direct-On-Line) connections
  • Star-delta configurations
  • Variable Frequency Drive (VFD) integration
  • Safety and related issues
Learning Outcomes: Commission and test a motor with DOL, star-delta, and VFD starting methods; apply electrical safety practices throughout
⚡ Live Drive System Testing
