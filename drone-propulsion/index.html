What the module is built to give you

Three things you will be able to do

Size the propulsion

Take any aircraft, its mass and its mission, and work out the thrust, torque and speed each motor must deliver.

Choose the right set

Pick the motor, propeller and battery that fit an aircraft, and explain why.

Design the motor

Go inside: slots, poles, windings, magnets and heat, modelled in FEMM and validated against test data.

One module, two depths

Choose how far inside the motor you go

Choose It is the on-ramp to Design It, so nobody starts from zero.

Track 1Choose It

For TVET and diploma students, hobbyists, competition teams, drone operators and service technicians. Basic electricity is enough.

  • Work out the thrust each motor must give for a given aircraft and mission.
  • Choose a motor, propeller and battery that fit the brief, and explain why.
  • Name every part inside a drone motor and say what it does.
  • Judge a set by thrust, efficiency and temperature.

Track 2Design It

For final-year and postgraduate students and industry engineers. Starts from Choose It or equivalent, plus basic electromagnetics.

  • Turn a mission into a motor specification: KV, torque, current, mass budget.
  • Choose the topology, the slot and pole combination and the winding.
  • Size and model an outer-rotor motor in FEMM and predict its heating over a mission.
  • Trade mass against losses and temperature through material choices.
  • Validate against test data and explain any gap.
Grounded in a real motor
