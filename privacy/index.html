Privacy

Privacy notice for free accounts

Updated 12 November 2026. What a free Motoreka account keeps, why, where, and how to delete it.

Who we are

Motoreka is a product of Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah (UMPSA), developed at the Electromagnetic Design Laboratory (EMDLab). UMPSA is responsible for the personal data described here, under Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010.

What we keep

  • From you: your email, your name as it should appear on the certificate, and, if you give them, your organisation and role.
  • From your learning: each answer you give to the questions in the lectures (both tries), where you stopped in each lecture, and your certificates.
  • About your account: when you signed up and signed in, and the link tag of your first visit (for example, that you came from LinkedIn).
  • Kept by the sign-in service for security: the internet address and browser used to sign in, to stop misuse.

Why

  • To run your account: sign you in, save your answers and progress, and issue your certificate.
  • To let anyone you share it with check your certificate: the check page shows your name, the course, your score and the date, and nothing else.
  • To improve the lectures, and for teaching research, using results that are combined and anonymised (for example, how many learners got a question right on the first try). No one is named.
  • To email you about new lectures and tools, at most once a month, only if you ticked that box. You can untick it at any time.

Where it is kept and who sees it

The account database is run for us by Supabase on servers in Singapore. Only the Motoreka team at EMDLab, UMPSA, can see learners' records. We do not sell or share your data with anyone else.

The lecture videos play from YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com), and only when you press play; YouTube's own privacy policy applies to the video. We count visits to motoreka.my with Umami, which uses no cookies and keeps no personal data.

How long

We keep your account until you delete it. We delete accounts that have not been used for three years. Certificates are kept as long as the account, so the check link keeps working.

Your rights

  • See and correct: your name, organisation and role are on your account page, where you can change them.
  • Delete: “Delete my account” on the same page deletes your account, your answers and your certificates at once.
  • Ask: for a copy of your data, or anything else about it, email mahizami@umpsa.edu.my.

Changes

If this notice changes in a way that matters, we tell account holders by email before the change applies.
