Slip Lab

Load the kit motor and watch the rotor fall behind the field. Slip, rotor current and torque change together, from FEMM runs of the motor students build.

Open Slip Lab
What you can do
  • Read torque against slip from FEMM solutions of the kit motor's cross-section
  • See the field in the motor at three slips
  • Follow the equivalent circuit and its power flow at any load
  • Turn no-load and locked-rotor test results into the motor's parameters
