Force

Seven FEMM demos of electromagnetism in one Windows app: the field around a wire, a coil with and without iron, permanent magnets, Faraday's law, the force on a conductor and an electromagnet pulling its armature. Set the inputs, see the model, solve it in FEMM and read the results.

The seven demos

From a wire to an electromagnet

Each demo is one session of MTM-01 Foundation, the first module of the Induction Motor Trainer. The workbook, the slides and the bench kit that go with them come with the trainer.

  1. 1.1Ampère Wire DemoThe field around a straight wire carrying DC: |B| against distance, FEMM against Ampère's law, inside and outside the conductor.
  2. 1.2Coil Inductance · air coreAn axisymmetric coil in air: inductance from the flux linkage at 1 A, |B| along the axis. Loads a parameter script if you have one.
  3. 1.2Coil Inductance · air and iron coreThe same coil with a cylindrical core of any FEMM library material: how much the iron raises L, and why not by μr.
  4. 1.3PM Flux SimulatorOne rectangular magnet, then two: the field map with field lines, |B| along the axis and B at the midpoint between the magnets.
  5. 1.4Faraday's Law SimulatorA coil turning between two magnet bands: flux linkage Ψ and EMF e = −dΨ/dt over one revolution, on a time axis.
  6. 1.5Lorentz Force & TorqueA one-turn coil between two magnet bands: the force on each conductor and the torque on the coil, at one angle or swept.
  7. 1.5Energy Method EI CoreAn EI electromagnet: the pull on the I-core and the inductance against the air gap; check F = ½ I² dL/dx.
Install

Three steps, no Python

  1. Download and run Force_Setup.exeIf Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  2. Keep “Set up FEMM 4.2” tickedSetup fetches FEMM 4.2, free software by David Meeker, from femm.info, checks it, installs it and links it to Force. Windows asks once for administrator rights.
  3. Open ForceFrom the Start menu or the desktop. Pick a session and open its demo.
