The problem

Students observe. They rarely build.

Most motor training kits are sealed, fixed units. Labs lack affordable hardware that students can open, so the inside of a motor stays a diagram.

  • Commercial training setsRM 50K – 100KSealed sets (Lucas Nülle, LabVolt) that cannot be taken apart: the price of a motor students never see inside.
  • Sealed constructionThe design stays hiddenStudents cannot see the slots, the end windings or the air gap, or measure a design choice against its result.
  • Design to operationOne path, end to endFew training systems take students from electromagnetic design through manufacture to drive operation on one machine.
The trainer

A motor that opens up

Stackable laminations

Students stack the core

A 24-slot stator built from loose M330-35A silicon steel sheets, stacked by hand.

Bolt-and-nut assembly

Built and rebuilt

Reversible construction instead of bonding: the motor is taken apart and assembled again, session after session.

Visible end windings

Physics you can see

The open frame shows the winding, the slots and the air gap that the trainees calculate.

In development

Interchangeable rotors

Permanent magnet and synchronous reluctance rotors for the same stator, so one kit teaches three machine types. Ask us if you want to pilot them.

Five modules

One motor, from prediction to measurement

Five modules follow one induction motor through one CDIO cycle. Trainees predict how the kit motor will perform, build and wind it, then run it and measure it against their prediction. Adopt all five, or the modules that fit your curriculum.

  1. 01MTM-01FoundationFields, iron, magnets, induction and force, in FEMM and on the benchReady
  2. 02MTM-02-IMInductionOpen the kit motor and predict its torque, current and efficiencyReady
  3. 03MTM-03-IMBuildStack the core, fit the cage rotor, assemble a motor that turns freelyReady
  4. 04MTM-04WindDesign, wind, insert and test the three-phase stator windingReady
  5. 05MTM-05-IMDrivePower it up from DOL, star-delta and a VFD, and measure itIn preparation
