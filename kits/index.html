01Learn · The modules

Five modules, notes and worksheets

MTM-01 Foundation, MTM-02 Core, MTM-03 Wind, MTM-04-IM Build and MTM-05-IM Drive: from electromagnetic theory through lamination design, winding and assembly to drive commissioning.

02Practice · The trainer and apps

A motor that opens up

A 24-slot stator stacked by hand, a three-phase winding wound by students, and a bolt-and-nut build taken apart and rebuilt every session. The Force, CoreSim and Winding Assistant apps go with it.
