Industry · EV Traction Motor

EV Traction Motor Sizing and Design

Two mornings for automotive and motor engineers: one running case, a range-extended B-segment car, from vehicle targets to a motor checked in FEMM.

  1. S0Why this courseWhat the traction motor does to range, battery size and fuel use
  2. S1Sizing from vehicle specificationTorque, speed and power from the vehicle; rotor diameter and stack length
  3. S2Motor topologies comparedIPM, induction, wound-field, SynRM and others, weighted for the application
  4. S3Components and materialsElectrical steel, magnet grades, windings and material cost
  5. S4System implicationsInverter, cooling, noise and mechanical limits
  6. S5FEMM torque labBuild the motor in FEMM and read its torque, ripple and back-EMF
  7. DRDesign reviewEach group defends its design card
