Where we stand

What we have today, and what we are building

Plainly: the training works and is recognised; the equipment to design, build and test motors at traction scale is what we are building next.

Today

  • The Induction Motor Trainer, taught to its first cohorts; CITREX 2026 Gold Award
  • The EV Traction Motor course: three modules, a companion book and six design tools
  • Motor sizing, finite element and thermal methods, published since 2013
  • Prototype work at EMDLab: laser-cut laminations, stator assembly and a test rig

Next, with partners

  • Test equipment for traction-class motors
  • Winding, hairpin and rotor build capability for prototypes
  • More trainers and presenters, so programmes can scale
  • Industry projects that turn training into design work
Ways to partner

Four ways to work with us

Fund training programmes

Sponsored cohorts: TVET students on the Induction Motor Trainer, technicians reskilled for electric drives, engineers on the EV Traction Motor course. We report who was trained, what they built and measured, and how they did.

Build equipment capability

Co-invest in test and prototype equipment at EMDLab, for motors of traction class. Your engineers and your sponsored trainees use it with ours.

Match us with industry

Introduce companies that need motor design studies, prototypes or trained staff. We scope the work with them and deliver it through UMPSA.

Place your students with us

Institutions send students and instructors for workshops, final-year projects and train-the-trainer at UMPSA, on our trainers and tools.

The plan

Training first, then design and building

Our mission

We train the people who will design, build and maintain electric motors in Malaysia: students, technicians and engineers, by having them build and design real machines.

Our vision

By 2030, Malaysian-trained engineers and technicians designing and building electric motors for our own vehicles and for export.

  1. Now · 2026

    Training people

    • The Induction Motor Trainer in TVET and university labs
    • The EV Traction Motor course for engineering teams
    • Free tools anyone can use
  2. Next · 2027

    Design studies and prototypes

    • Motor design studies for industry, contracted through UMPSA
    • Prototype stators and rotors built and tested at EMDLab
    • More trainers and presenters, to train at scale
  3. Then · to 2030

    Building motors with partners

    • Test and prototype equipment built up with industry partners
    • Malaysian-trained teams designing motors for local vehicles and export
